Account Architecture & Transparency

Difference Between a Buyer's Account & a Farmer's Account

AgriConnect is designed with distinct workflows tailored specifically to consumers purchasing fresh produce and agricultural producers supplying crops.

🛒

Buyer's Account

For Consumers, Families & Businesses
Zero Hassle

A buyer simply needs to provide complete details to make a purchase and is not required to link an e-wallet or e-banking account.

  • ✓ Complete Shipment Details: Provide recipient full name, exact delivery address, active contact numbers, and delivery instructions.
  • ✓ No Account Linking Required: No need to connect bank accounts or digital wallets. Pay comfortably via Cash on Delivery (COD) or scan QR upon arrival.
  • ✓ Profile Activity Display: Profile conveniently tracks your current purchases in transit, total spending, and verified purchase history.
Summary: Pure convenience. Enter shipping coordinates, choose fresh harvests, and track real-time delivery without financial credentials setup.
🌾

Farmer's Account

For Growers, Planters & Cooperatives
Accredited

A farmer must upload specific requirements and accreditation documents to establish credibility and reliability, and can link digital accounts for direct disbursements.

  • ✓ Requirements & Accreditation: Must upload DA-RSBSA certificates, PhilGAP accreditation, and farm clearances to prove authenticity.
  • ✓ Linked Payout Channels: Farmers can link an e-banking or e-wallet account (LandBank, GCash, Maya) with QR codes to receive 100% direct buyer payments.
  • ✓ Profile Operational Display: Profile displays farm geo-tags, GPS coordinates, elevation, crop specializations, and DA verification badge.
Summary: Maximum trust. Verified credentials protect buyer confidence, while linked payout channels ensure zero platform cuts for farmers.
