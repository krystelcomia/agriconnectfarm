Help & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

How do delivery fees and cold-chain transport work?

We operate scheduled cold-chain delivery routes linking Benguet, Nueva Ecija, Cavite, and Laguna directly with Metro Manila and major urban centers. Delivery is a transparent flat fee (₱95 for standard household packages) or calculated by freight weight for commercial restaurant volume.

Can restaurants, hotels, or supermarkets purchase in bulk?

Yes! Commercial buyers can place orders by the crate, sack (50kg), or weekly recurring subscription. Simply select the available unit or click "Meet the Farmers" to coordinate directly with the producer.

How does AgriConnect verify that a farmer is genuine?

Every grower on AgriConnect undergoes an on-site validation check through local municipal agricultural offices (MAO) and Department of Agriculture registries. We verify land titles/leases, farming practices, and identity.

Can I visit the farm in person and buy directly at the farm gate?

Yes! Our Maps section provides exact coordinates and expressway driving directions for each farm. We recommend notifying the farmer via their contact info beforehand.
