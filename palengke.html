How AgriConnect Works

A simple, secure connection designed for both digital-first buyers and traditional rural farmers.

1

Farmer Lists Harvest

Farmers post their expected harvest with photos, per-kilo/sack prices, and available stocks with AI price guidance.

Households, eateries, and cooperatives order directly from their preferred farm with custom quantities.
