How it works

Farmers → AgriConnect → Buyers

A simple, familiar flow from harvest to handover — designed to be easy even for first-time users of online selling.

STEP 1

Farmer lists product

Upload a photo, set price per kilo, sack or piece, and the available quantity.

STEP 2

Buyer discovers it

Buyers search and filter by product, category, price, location and availability.

STEP 3

Order is placed

Add to cart, choose the quantity, then checkout for pickup or delivery.

STEP 4

Farmer confirms

The farmer accepts the order and prepares the harvest. Stock updates automatically.

STEP 5

Pickup or delivery

Track each stage of the order until it reaches the buyer.

STEP 6

Order completed

The buyer reviews the transaction and can reorder anytime.
