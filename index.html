Philippine Agricultural Reality

The Farmer's Situation & Why Direct Trade Matters

In the Philippines, smallholder farmers cultivate and supply over 75% of our domestic food supply, yet traditional supply chains leave them vulnerable to extreme price suppression, high post-harvest losses, and multi-tier middleman extraction.

Middleman Price Suppression

Traditional consolidators purchase highland cabbage at ₱25–₱30/kg at farmgate, yet consumers in Metro Manila pay ₱120–₱150/kg. Up to 75% of the consumer peso is captured by intermediaries rather than the growers who bear all crop risks.

30%–40% Post-Harvest Spoilage

Due to delayed transfers across informal trading posts and lack of temperature control, nearly a third of all fresh harvest rots before reaching retail markets, directly cutting into farmer income and raising retail grocery prices.

The AgriConnect Direct Solution

We connect farmers directly to households and culinary businesses. By eliminating intermediary markups, farmers receive 85%–92% of the total purchase, while buyers receive freshly harvested crops dispatched within 24 hours.
